About

Why we built the journal. And why the community followed.

We built a 12-week guided journal because the gap between knowing and doing is one of the most well-documented phenomena in behavioral science, and one of the least well-served by the tools available to most people. The knowledge exists in abundance. Access to most of it is the problem.

Cost. Availability. Geography. Stigma. These are the reasons people sit with the full awareness of what they need to change and still cannot reach the support that would help them do it. They decide the tools are not for them. That change is for other people. People who have more time, more money, more access.

Drawing on behavioral science and years of working alongside coaches and facilitators, we built the journal to change that. It respects your time. It asks only for honesty. And it assumes the intelligence of the person holding it.

We built the community because this kind of work changes how people see themselves, and that change wants to be shared with others who would understand.

Nobody finishes the twelve weeks quite the same way. What you’ll take with you is a language of your own: a precise, private vocabulary that lets you name the patterns that used to run you, the gaps in your behavior, and the moments just before the old response took over. You can share a language like that with the people who cross your path. But it’s something else to say “I caught myself negotiating again” to a room full of people who simply nod. They’ve caught themselves too, halfway through an excuse or a “just this once.”

MyJournal Community is that room. It is for people who have finished the twelve weeks, and for those who are deciding whether to begin.
